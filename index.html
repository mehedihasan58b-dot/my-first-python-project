How to play · Custom rules

Player 1 starts the first match; the first turn alternates only after a win or draw. Restarting an unfinished match keeps the same starter. Top starts with three pieces, bottom with three; the middle row is empty. Select your piece, then a highlighted adjacent empty point connected by a line. No jumping. Only the middle row (3,4,5), middle column (1,4,7) and diagonals (0,4,8), (2,4,6) win. Outer rows and columns never win. A blocked player loses. Three repetitions of the same board and turn, or 100 moves, draw. Restart restores the six original pieces. Back to Setup ends the round without changing scores.

Audio starts after your first tap. Music is optional.
