Scores for this mode and difficulty · this page session

Three pieces. One straight line.
All six pieces start on the board: three red (X) and three blue (O). Red starts. Select your piece and tap a highlighted adjacent empty point on a connecting line. No jumping. All three rows, columns and the two long diagonals are winning lines. A blocked player loses. Three repetitions of the same board and turn, or 100 movement turns, draw. In computer mode you play Red (X). Changing settings starts a new round.

No sign-ups. Just good company.
