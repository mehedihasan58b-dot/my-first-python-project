Scores for this mode and difficulty · this page session

Three pieces. One straight line.
X starts. Each player places exactly three pieces, one per turn (an earlier line wins immediately). Then select your piece and tap a highlighted adjacent empty point on a connecting line. No jumping. All three rows, columns and the two long diagonals are winning lines. A blocked player loses. Three repetitions of the same board and turn, or 100 movement turns, draw. In computer mode you play X. Changing settings starts a new round.

No sign-ups. Just good company.
